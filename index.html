About

I'm a PhD candidate in Chemistry at MIT, co-advised by Prof. Laura Kiessling and Prof. Alex Shalek. Glycans coat every cell, yet they are among the hardest molecules to measure in tissue. My work develops multimodal single-cell and spatial platforms that read out functional glycosylation alongside protein and mRNA, using DNA-barcoded human recombinant lectins.

With these tools I've mapped how lectin-binding glycan patterns shift during immune activation in blood, uncovered cancer-associated glycan niches in follicular lymphoma with diagnostic value, and charted how epithelial cells shape immune and microbial communities in oral inflammatory disease.

I trained first as a physical chemist at IISER Kolkata (B.S.–M.S.), working on perovskite nanowires and single-particle tracking, and on MRI pulse sequences at the Weizmann Institute. In January 2027 I'll join David Baker's lab at the Institute for Protein Design, University of Washington, as a postdoctoral fellow, working at the interface of biology and AI.

Research

Measuring the sugar layer of human cells in tissue context, and using it to understand immunity and disease.

GlycoScope: functional glycan profiling at single-cell and spatial resolution

A multimodal platform pairing DNA-barcoded human recombinant lectins with protein and mRNA readouts, so glycan binding can be measured in the same cell and the same tissue section as everything else.

The bigger picture

Glycans control how immune cells recognize self from non-self, yet standard omics leave them out. Applied to human blood and lymphoma, GlycoScope revealed glycan shifts tied to immune activation and distinct, diagnostically informative glycan niches in follicular lymphoma.

Basak*, Ortiz-Cordero*, Yiu* et al., bioRxiv 2026 →

Oral mucosal inflammation

Single-cell and spatial atlases of periodontitis, peri-implantitis and human fungiform taste buds, asking how epithelial and stromal cells organize the immune and microbial life around them.

The bigger picture

The oral mucosa sits at a constant interface with microbes. Mapping which cells recruit which immune populations in health versus inflammation points to cell-type-specific targets for therapy design.

Two first-author manuscripts in preparation.

Spatial multi-omics & glyco-immunology

Collaborative work integrating many molecular layers on the same tissue slide, and on how carbohydrate–lectin interactions can reprogram dendritic cells for anti-tumor immunity.

The bigger picture

Combining glycan, protein and transcript measurements in space moves us toward a computationally complete picture of the tumor microenvironment.

Cancer Discovery 2026 · ACS Nano 2024

Physical chemistry roots

Before biology: synthesis, optical characterization and single-particle tracking of CsPbBr3 perovskite nanowires and quantum dots at IISER Kolkata, and pulse-sequence design for diffusion MRI and solid-state NMR at the Weizmann Institute.

The bigger picture

A quantitative, measurement-first way of thinking that carries into how I design biological assays today.

Otikovs, Basak & Frydman, Magn. Reson. Imaging 2022 →
