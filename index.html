About

I'm a PhD candidate in Chemistry at MIT, co-advised by Prof. Laura Kiessling and Prof. Alex Shalek. Glycans coat every cell, yet they are among the hardest molecules to measure in tissue. My work develops multimodal single-cell and spatial platforms that read out functional glycosylation alongside protein and mRNA, using DNA-barcoded human recombinant lectins.

With these tools I've mapped how lectin-binding glycan patterns shift during immune activation in blood, uncovered cancer-associated glycan niches in follicular lymphoma with diagnostic value, and charted how epithelial and stromal cells shape immune and microbial communities in oral inflammatory disease.

I trained previously as a physical chemist at IISER Kolkata (B.S.–M.S.), working on perovskite nanowires and single-particle tracking, and on MRI and NMR pulse sequence design at the Weizmann Institute

Research

Building tools and employing them to understand cells in their native tissue context across health and disease.

GOAT-seq and GlycoScope: first-ever functional glycan profiling in human tissues at single-cell and spatial resolution

A multimodal platform pairing DNA-barcoded human recombinant lectins with protein and mRNA readouts, so glycan binding can be measured in the same cell and the same tissue section as everything else leading to a comprehensive understanding of the human lectin interactome in tissues.

The bigger picture

Glycans control how immune cells recognize self from non-self, yet standard omics leave them out. Applied to human blood and lymphoma, GOAT-seq and GlycoScope revealed glycan shifts tied to immune activation and distinct, diagnostically informative glycan niches in follicular lymphoma.

Basak*, Ortiz-Cordero*, Yiu* et al., bioRxiv 2026 →

Oral mucosal inflammation

Single-cell and spatial atlases of periodontitis, peri-implantitis and human fungiform taste buds, asking how epithelial and stromal cells organize the immune and microbial life around them in homeostasis and inflammation.

The bigger picture

The oral mucosa sits at a constant interface with microbes. Mapping which cells recruit which immune populations in health versus inflammation points to cell-type-specific targets for therapy design.

Two first-author manuscripts in preparation.

Glycan-based vaccines & glyco-immunology

Virus-like particles decorated with mannosylated glycans engage dendritic-cell lectins, are taken up into the endosome, and signal through TLR7 — turning a carbohydrate display into the immune adjuvant itself.

The bigger picture

Glycan density and spacing on a particle use lectin-binding internalisation on dendritic cells to decide how immune system behaves after vaccination. Tuning that display using mannosylated virus-like particles reprograms DCs toward type 1 anti-tumor immunity — a vaccine design problem solved with carbohydrates.

Lensch, Gabba, … Basak, … Kiessling, ACS Nano 2024 →

Physical chemistry roots

Synthesis, optical characterisation and single-particle tracking of CsPbBr3 perovskite nanowires and quantum dots at IISER Kolkata, and pulse-sequence design for diffusion-weighted MRI and solid-state NMR at the Weizmann Institute.

The bigger picture

Developing wetlab and computational workflows for better resolution super-resolution imaging and tracking as well as MRI image reconstruction from noisy, undersampled data using regularization algorithms.

Otikovs, Basak & Frydman, Magn. Reson. Imaging 2022 →
